## Scope

One discoverable adapter, full pinned upstream submodule, isolated and all/figures profiles,
registry, routing, provenance, and changelog. Other Baoyu skills are not projected or executed.
The adapter loads upstream references from their real directory, recommends scientific style
for scientific requests, and adds checks for anatomy, labels, evidence, and causal direction.
Runtime instructions and existing user authorization take precedence over upstream examples.

## Validation and privacy

Installation uses no image APIs or upstream executable helpers. Article Illustrator itself
contains Markdown instructions and reference files; unrelated provider test suites are outside
this integration. No credentials, private scientific sources, reference images, or outputs
are added. The central privacy scan excludes submodules; it is not an upstream-wide audit.
Structural validation and link verification are recorded below after execution.
